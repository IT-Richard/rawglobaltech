Hospital Management System

Our comprehensive Hospital Management System digitises patient care, clinical workflows, billing and administration — helping clinics and hospitals deliver safer, faster and more connected healthcare.

Patient Management

  • Patient Registration & Records — Capture complete demographic, contact and insurance details in a unified profile.
  • Medical History & Allergies — Track past diagnoses, allergies, chronic conditions and immunisations.
  • Appointment Scheduling — Book, reschedule and manage appointments across departments and doctors.
  • Bed & Ward Management — Monitor bed availability, admissions, transfers and discharges in real time.
  • Patient Portal — Let patients view records, appointments and results securely online.

Clinical & Electronic Health Records

  • Electronic Health Records (EHR) — Centralised digital charts accessible to authorised clinicians.
  • Consultation & Notes — Structured doctor notes, diagnosis and treatment plans per visit.
  • Prescription Management — Generate, print and track digital prescriptions with dosage guidance.
  • Lab & Diagnostics — Order tests, capture results and link them directly to patient charts.
  • Vital Signs Tracking — Record and trend temperature, blood pressure, pulse and oxygen over time.

Billing & Finance

  • Billing & Invoicing — Auto-generate itemised bills for consultations, procedures and admissions.
  • Insurance Claims — Manage cover verification, claims submission and reimbursements.
  • Payment Processing — Accept cash, card and mobile payments with instant receipts.
  • Financial Reports — Revenue, expense and outstanding-balance reports for management.

Pharmacy & Inventory

  • Pharmacy Dispensing — Fill prescriptions and track dispensed medication against patient visits.
  • Drug Stock & Expiry — Monitor stock levels, batches and expiry dates to prevent wastage.
  • Supplier Management — Manage vendors, purchase orders and restocking workflows.

Staff & Administration

  • Doctor & Staff Management — Maintain credentials, departments and schedules for every user.
  • Duty Roster — Plan shifts, on-call rotations and ward assignments.
  • Role-Based Access Control — Secure data with distinct admin, doctor, nurse and receptionist roles.
  • Reports & Analytics — OPD/IPD statistics, patient flow and operational dashboards.

Communication & Alerts

  • SMS & Email Notifications — Send appointment reminders and result alerts to patients.
  • Emergency Alerts — Flag critical cases and notify the right team instantly.

Why Choose Our System

  • Cloud-based, secure and accessible from any device.
  • Designed for clinics, hospitals and specialised centres.
  • HIPAA-aware, with role-based security and audit trails.
  • Customisable modules and lifetime updates included.

Frequently Asked Questions

How does RAW GLOBAL TECH handle Hospital Management System deployment?

We deliver the system as a secure, cloud-based platform accessible from any device, with role-based access for admins, doctors, nurses and receptionists. Deployment is tailored to your facility — we handle setup, data migration, staff onboarding and training, and ongoing updates. On-premise options and custom modules are available on request.

Is patient data secure?

Yes. The system uses role-based access control, secure cloud hosting and full audit trails so only authorised clinicians can view patient records, and every action is logged.

Can the system manage pharmacy and billing together?

Yes. It includes pharmacy dispensing, drug stock and expiry tracking, supplier management, as well as billing, insurance claims and payment processing in one integrated workflow.
