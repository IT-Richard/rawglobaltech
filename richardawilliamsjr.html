About Me

I am a technology leader, Network Specialist and cybersecurity practitioner with more than seven years of experience delivering innovative, secure solutions for government, business, healthcare, education and faith-based organisations. As the Founder and CEO of RAW GLOBAL TECH, LLC, my focus is simple: I help organisations use technology to work smarter, stay secure, and grow with confidence.

I specialise in network administration, IT infrastructure, cybersecurity, cloud solutions, systems administration and software development. Over the years I have designed, deployed and maintained secure environments supporting hundreds of users — while guiding leadership teams through digital transformation and stronger information security.

My hands-on expertise spans Local Area Networks, Windows Server and Active Directory, AWS cloud services, vulnerability assessments, penetration testing, website and application development, CCTV, VoIP, firewall management and enterprise support. I have also led the build of custom software — including Human Resource and Healthcare Management Systems — that help organisations run more efficiently and serve people better.

Beyond technology, I bring proven leadership as a Project Field Coordinator, managing cross-functional teams and complex initiatives from planning to delivery. I have taught computer literacy and IT fundamentals and served as a mentor and community leader — experience that shapes how I communicate, collaborate and deliver results for my clients.

Alongside my work in technology, I serve as the Senior Pastor of Mount Sinai Temple Church (GCMCI), where I lead, teach and care for my congregation. That pastoral calling shapes how I listen, lead teams and serve others with integrity — values I bring into every client relationship.

Driven by excellence, innovation and continuous learning, I pair deep technical skill with clear leadership and problem-solving — delivering secure, scalable and reliable solutions that help businesses and institutions thrive in a fast-changing digital world.
