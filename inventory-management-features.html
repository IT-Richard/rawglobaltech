Inventory Management System

Our Inventory Management System gives you real-time control over stock, purchasing, sales and warehouses — so you always know what you have, what you need and what it's worth.

Product & Catalogue

  • Product Management — Maintain a full catalogue with descriptions, images and pricing.
  • Categories & SKUs — Organise items with categories, brands and unique SKU codes.
  • Barcode Generation — Create and print barcodes for fast scanning and checkout.
  • Multi-Warehouse — Track stock across multiple locations and stores from one dashboard.

Stock Control

  • Real-Time Stock Tracking — Live quantity updates on every issue and receipt.
  • Purchase Orders — Create, approve and receive purchase orders with suppliers.
  • Goods Receiving — Log incoming shipments and auto-update inventory levels.
  • Stock Transfers — Move items between warehouses with full traceability.
  • Adjustments & Audits — Correct discrepancies with reason-coded adjustments.

Sales & Orders

  • Sales Orders — Raise orders, fulfil and invoice from a single workflow.
  • Point of Sale — Fast, barcode-driven checkout for retail counters.
  • Quotations — Generate and convert quotes into confirmed orders.
  • Customer Management — Track customers, history and outstanding balances.

Suppliers & Procurement

  • Supplier Management — Store vendor contacts, terms and performance.
  • Procurement Workflow — Streamline requesting, approval and purchasing.
  • Reorder Levels — Auto-flag items that fall below minimum stock thresholds.

Reporting & Valuation

  • Stock Reports — On-hand, committed and available quantities at a glance.
  • Inventory Valuation — Calculate stock value by FIFO, LIFO or average cost.
  • Low-Stock & Expiry Alerts — Never run out or waste expiring stock.
  • Audit Trail — Complete log of every stock movement for accountability.

Why Choose Our System

  • Cloud-based, real-time and mobile-friendly.
  • Built for retail, distribution and warehouses.
  • Live dashboards and instant low-stock alerts.
  • Integrates with sales, accounting and lifetime updates.
